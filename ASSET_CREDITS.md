# Asset provenance

## Mission Brief soundtrack

`public/audio/mission-brief.mp3` is a synthetic cash-register soundscape
generated specifically for BankMan with a publicly available online audio
generation tool.

The prompt requested generic sounds associated with a cash register, coins,
and a checkout drawer. The file does not contain samples, copied audio, or
extracted fragments from Pink Floyd's “Money” or any other commercial
recording.

The soundtrack is distributed under the repository's portfolio-only usage
terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
